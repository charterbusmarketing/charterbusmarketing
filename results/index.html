Takeovers: what we found

Audits of existing accounts when we got access. Before-and-after results will follow.

Account A · Charter bus operator · Mid-Atlantic

$2,254 spent. Zero leads recorded.

Audit · Nov 2026
What we found
$2,254spent on one Performance Max campaign
9,806clicks, at about $0.23 each
0conversions recorded
  • One Performance Max campaign, set to "maximize conversions," ran for about five months.
  • It brought in lots of very cheap clicks, the kind that rarely turn into charter bookings.
  • No quote request or phone call was ever recorded as a conversion, so Google had nothing real to aim for.
  • After that, the account sat dark for most of a year.
What we changed
  • Paused the old campaign and built 5 Search campaigns by trip type: charter rental, corporate & events, sports & group trips, weddings, and brand.
  • Rebuilt tracking so only real leads count: website quote requests, calls of 90 seconds or more, and qualified calls from ads.
  • Small, controlled budgets per trip type while the new campaigns learn.
So far: 3 real quote requests from the first $324 of spend.
Account B · Charter & shuttle operator · California

Thousands of "conversions." Most weren't leads.

Audit · Nov 2026
What we found (last 2 years)
6 figuresof ad spend
~30%reported conversion rate (not realistic for charter)
~72%of recorded conversions were clicks, not leads
  • Clicks on the "Get Quote" button, phone-number taps, email clicks and map actions were counted alongside real quote requests and calls.
  • Dozens of conversion actions had piled up over the years, including test actions and old Universal Analytics goals.
  • One catch-all campaign took about two-thirds of the budget and was limited by budget.
  • Lots of short-lived, one-off event campaigns. One reported "conversions" at under $2 each.
What we're fixing
  • Count only real leads (submitted quotes and qualified calls), so the reports and Google's bidding go after actual bookings.
  • Clean up the conversion list and retire dead and test actions.
  • Split the catch-all budget by trip type and service area.
  • Swap one-off event campaigns for evergreen ones by trip type, with seasonal ad groups inside them.

How we count

So the numbers mean something.

Submitted quote requests and qualified calls. Button clicks, page views and phone-number taps never count as leads.

"Per lead from Google Ads" counts only leads that came straight from an ad. "Every website quote request" counts all leads against the same ad spend.
