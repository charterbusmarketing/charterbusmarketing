Client A · Charter bus operator · Mid-Atlantic

$2,254 spent. Zero leads recorded.

Rebuilt Nov 2026 · results due Feb 2027
Before
$2,254spent on one Performance Max campaign
9,806clicks, at about $0.23 each
0conversions recorded
  • One Performance Max campaign at $30/day, set to "maximize conversions," ran from about August to December 2025.
  • It brought in lots of very cheap clicks, the kind that rarely turn into charter bookings.
  • No quote request or phone call was ever recorded as a conversion, so Google had nothing real to aim for.
  • After that, the account sat dark for most of 2026.
What we changed
  • Paused the old campaign and built 5 Search campaigns by trip type: charter bus rental, corporate & events, sports & group trips, weddings, and brand.
  • Rebuilt tracking so only real leads count: website quote requests, calls of 90 seconds or more, and qualified calls from ads. Page views and phone-number clicks are tracked but don't count as leads.
  • Small, controlled budgets per trip type ($3–15/day) while the new campaigns learn.
So far: 3 real quote requests from the first $324 of spend.

Client B · Charter & shuttle operator · SF Bay Area

10,724 "conversions." Most weren't leads.

Taken over Nov 2026 · results due Feb–Mar 2027
Before (last 2 years)
$156.5kad spend
29.7%reported conversion rate (not realistic for charter)
~72%of recorded conversions were clicks, not leads
  • Clicks on the "Get Quote" button (6,579), phone-number taps (2,037), email clicks and map actions were counted alongside real quote requests and calls.
  • Over the years 43 conversion actions had piled up, including test actions and old Universal Analytics goals.
  • One catch-all campaign took about two-thirds of all spend and was limited by budget.
  • 52 campaigns in total, many of them short-lived one-offs (Super Bowl, ski trips, the World Cup). One Performance Max campaign showed 3,592 "conversions" at $1.58 each.
The plan
  • Count only real leads as conversions (submitted quotes and qualified calls), so the reports and Google's bidding go after actual bookings.
  • Clean up the conversion list and retire dead and test actions.
  • Split the catch-all budget by trip type and service area, so each one gets what it earns.
  • Swap one-off event campaigns for evergreen ones by trip type, with seasonal ad groups inside them.
Starting point (last 90 days): about $18.5k spend and 2,586 clicks across 4 active campaigns.

How we measure
